the Tündük ring

A tündük is the wooden ring at the top of the yurt: all the poles meet in it, and the smoke goes out and the light comes in. This webring is the same idea: every nest is a pole, the ring holds them together. You click "next" and you go around the yurt.

Rules: (1) a real nest on uyalar.net, made by you, not a template you didn't touch; (2) the ring widget on your main page; (3) be kind in other people's guestbooks; (4) read point 5 of the Uyalar rules, and then think about it for a minute. Nobody wants the empty-nest page.
