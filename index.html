Build your nest on the Internet!

Uyalar (uyalar, «nests») is free hosting for home pages, made in Irad. Your own site at uyalar.net/yourname, 20 MB, a file manager in the browser and FTP, the TOP-100 rating and a place in the catalogue. We do not put advertising inside your text: only one banner at the top of the page pays for the server.
