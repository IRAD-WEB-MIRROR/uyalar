Discography

Talas Sunrise (1995)
Ak Jol Records, cassette. Recorded in the IradOne radio studio by Joldoshbek Kasymbekov of the Swift Hooves Hour. THE FIRST country-folk album of Irad!! Iradi versions of the Swift Hooves songs.
Ak Jol (1998)
Cassette. My favourite is the title song.
Zhylky Jyly (2002) — "Year of the Horse"
Cassette and CD (!!). The CD is very rare, I have only the cassette.

  • Talant Asan — banjo
  • Erkin Mamyrov — kyl-kyak, komuz
