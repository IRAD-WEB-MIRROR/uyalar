Welcome to the site of the Jon-Aryk Secondary School! / Добро пожаловать!

Our school was opened in 1938, one year after the founding of the village by Korean families who came to the lower Chu to grow rice. Now it has 176 pupils in 11 classes, 19 teachers, a computer class with 8 computers (since 2006, from the program of the Department of Education) and a sports hall. Teaching is in Russian, with the Iradi language from the 1st class and English from the 3rd. Korean is an optional subject.

Director: Lyudmila Petrovna Tsoi.
