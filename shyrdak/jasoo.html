how to use it like apa

Start with the ram's horn, four to the middle, red and dark blue. That's the carpet in every house. Then break the rules. "Two felts" swaps the colours in every second square: that's what a real shyrdak looks like when you cut two felts at once and mix the pieces. The cord colour is the third colour, the one that holds everything together.
