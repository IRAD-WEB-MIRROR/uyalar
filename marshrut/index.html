Talas.

Salam. I'm Adilet, people say adik. I live in the Station District, I study roads and transport at TSU, and on Sundays I ride a route from the first stop to the last one without getting off. This is the page about that.

Talas has 9 municipal bus routes (71 buses, the Talas City Bus Park, Drivers' Street 7) and 126 marshrutka routes in the 2026 register, of them 120 running, 2 som a ride. Nobody has a map of all of them on one page. The Bus Park site has a map, but it shows one route at a time. So I drew mine. It's below: the nine buses and the two marshrutkas I ride most (22 and 40). Press a number to see one line alone.

During the long one the foreign transit forums didn't open, and I had nothing to read on the 22. So I started counting our own routes instead. That was 2025. I'm at 60 of 129.

today's line

winter timetable from the first snow (Mayor's order 418-r, 1 Dec at the latest): buses start 15 min later, finish ~40 min earlier. Winter tyre checks on marshrutkas from 16 Nov. Station road closed 4-8 Nov: the 66 and bus 10 go round by Alikhan.
