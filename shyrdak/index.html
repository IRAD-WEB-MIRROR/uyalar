Kosh keldiñiz! Welcome!

I'm Aijan. I'm from Almaluu, the village of apples and felt above Talas, and I study textile at the Ayyubov art college in town. This nest belongs to me and to my grandmother, Burul-apa, who has made shyrdak since she was fourteen. She doesn't use the internet. I show her this page on my phone and she says "the red is wrong". Then she says "good".

A shyrdak is a felt carpet made of cut pieces: you make two felts in two colours, lay them one on the other, cut the pattern through both, swap the pieces, and sew them together with a cord along every seam. So one cutting gives two carpets, each the other one's negative. Nothing is wasted. That's the whole idea, and I still think it's magic.
