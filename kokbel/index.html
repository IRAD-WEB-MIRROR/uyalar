Hi everybody and welcome to my Kök Bel page!!!

My name is Chynara, I am 19, I study at TSU (economics, boring :) and I love Kök Bel more than anything in the world. My papa had the cassette Talas Sunrise in the car and I grew up singing "Talas Üchün Chap" before I could read.

Kök Bel ("Blue Pass") is the first country-folk band of Irad: banjo, guitar, kyl-kyak and komuz, and songs about horses, roads and Talas. They are not famous in America but they should be!!!

On this site: the lyrics (I write them by ear, sorry for mistakes), the discography, and my guestbook. Please sign it!!
