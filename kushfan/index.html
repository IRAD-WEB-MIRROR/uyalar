✦ welcome balapan ✦

hiii and welcome to my nest ✦ this is a fan shrine to KARAKUSH (Каракуш, "golden eagle"), five girls from Talas, the best Mor-Pop group in the world and the only one that matters, sorry 4Muuz.

it looks like 2003 on purpose. my older cousin showed me screenshots of her friend's fan site from back then, with the sparkles and the music that started by itself, and I said: that. I want that. (no music that starts by itself though. you're welcome.)

we call ourselves Balapan (балапан = eaglet, a baby eagle) because if they are the eagle then we are the little ones in the nest, waiting with our mouths open for content. it's not official. it should be.

✦ who are they (for new balapan)

Karakush debuted in 2018 with Uchuu (that is where the word balapan comes from, the eaglet in it). they are from Kök Ay Media in Talas (the blue moon agency). in February 2021 they released the EP Ak Börü ("White Wolf") and the video on the frozen lake with the white wolf capes and the horns, and the dance challenge went everywhere, Almaty, Tashkent, Korea even. in September 2021 the first full album Uya ("The Nest", yes like this site!!): the five EP songs, Jeyren, Ekranda and three new ones (Uya, Childe, Chuseok). best-selling album, Altyn-Zhylky 2021. they promoted in Korea in 2022. then Betkap (2022) and Passenger Seat (2023). Altyn-Zhylky in 2019, 2021 and 2023. foreigners call them a one-hit wonder. foreigners can be quiet.
