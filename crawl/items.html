items (the ones that matter)

Potions and the like are unidentified when you find them: they have a random look each game (horn, felt-wrapped, birch-bark, blue glass, walnut-wood...), so "the clay one" is kymyz in one run and kara suu in the next. how i identify potions (no spoilers, just maths)

1. The most common look in the first two valleys is nearly always kymyz.
2. A stack of 3+ that isn't kymyz is often ak süt.
3. Drink unknowns when you're at full HP, rested, with nothing in view and no river next to you (confusion + river = swim lesson).
4. Never drink-ID during the ride home with the Ene Malka. The valleys come after you, remember.
