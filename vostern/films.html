THE FILMS

Desert Sun's Rebellion

1979 · dir. Nurlan Sadikov · ∩∩∩∩∩

Where it all starts. 1922: Daniyar and five men steal the clan's horses back from Kara-Bek's caravanserai, at night, almost thirty minutes with no words. Then they turn on each other in the sands. The salt flat at noon. Daniyar alone with the herd and you don't know whose side he is on. Sadikov said he didn't even like westerns. He invented ours anyway.

The Golden Bay

1980 · dir. Timur Aslanov · ∩∩∩∩·

Rival clans race a prize stallion. Aslanov was supposed to direct Desert Sun's Rebellion and was replaced, and this is his answer. Kara-Bek (Sultanbekov) is here, cheating at the race in the most elegant way.

Desert Blue

1981 · dir. Karimov · ∩∩∩··

Gulnara Alimbekova in the lead. Slow, beautiful, too much sand in my opinion.

Guide of the Sands

1981 · dir. Adil Rakhimov · ∩∩∩∩·

A wanderer with no name helps a woman escape a whole army of bandits in the desert. Asylbekov's camera: every frame is a painting.

Track of the Snow Leopard

1983 · dir. Kairat Amankulov · ∩∩∩∩·

A border tracker after smugglers over the Tian Shan passes. Snow instead of sand, for once. Jumabaev's music.

The Silver Horseshoe

1984 · dir. Adil Rakhimov · ∩∩∩∩∩

The comedy one: a kolkhoz bookkeeper is mistaken for a legendary avenger. Almaz Sarybaev appears and parodies his own Daniyar. My father knows every line. The biggest hit of the decade at home.

Vengeance Rider

1986 · dir. Malik Shakirov · ∩∩∩∩∩

The last great Eastern. A rider's family is killed by a warlord (Kara-Bek, of course) and the whole film is the ride for revenge. Fast like nothing else from that time. Shakirov died in Talas in 1992, in his home. Watch it for him.

Journey of the Steppes

1988 · dir. Kairat Amankulov · ∩∩∩∩·

A young horse trainer lost in a sandstorm, three horses, bandits behind him. The end of the cycle. You can feel the eighties ending.

∩ = one horseshoe. Five horseshoes = watch it tonight.
