Rules of Uyalar

  1. One person, one nest. A club or a school can have one more.
  2. Do not remove or cover the banner at the top of your pages. It pays for the server.
  3. It is forbidden to place: pornography; viruses and cracks; other people's music and films for download; advertising of casinos and pills; materials against the laws in force in the territory of Irad.
  4. A nest that nobody opens for one year can be closed. Write to us before and we will keep it.
  5. The administration can close a nest without explanation when the law requires it.
  6. The administration is not responsible for the content of the nests. Their authors are.

If you see a bad nest

Write to abuse@uyalar.net with the address of the page. We look at the letters every week, not every day, we are only two people.

  • Points 2 and 3: we write to the author of the nest and give 7 days to fix it. If it is not fixed, the nest is closed.
  • Viruses, pornography and point 5: the nest is closed at once.
  • A closed nest shows a page "This nest is closed". Its files are kept for 30 days, the author can ask for them by e-mail.
  • Other people's photos or texts: write to us as the owner of the photo or the text, we pass your letter to the author of the nest.

Rules of 01.03.2007, point 5 added 15.11.2023.
