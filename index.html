Build your nest on the Internet!

Uyalar (uyalar, «nests») is free hosting for home pages, made in Irad. Your own site at uyalar.net/yourname, 20 MB, a file manager in the browser and FTP, the TOP-100 rating and a place in the catalogue. We do not put advertising inside your text: only one banner at the top of the page pays for the server.

News of Uyalar

14.03.2019 Uyalar is 14 years old! Thank you to everyone who built a nest with us, and to everyone who still updates it. Sign-up works as before; the forum stays read-only.

05.09.2016 The server has moved. Some sites did not open from 1 to 4 September; everything is back. Please keep a copy of your site on your own computer.

10.02.2014 The forum is closed for new messages because of spam (4,000 adverts for pills in one night). Old topics can be read.

20.11.2008 New design of Uyalar! The TOP-100 is now counted every hour, and every nest has its own counter. Take the code in Help.

01.10.2007 We are back. The server was switched off for 41 days this summer. All sites are safe. Thank you for waiting and for your letters.

01.03.2007 Your nest is now 20 MB instead of 10. FTP access for everybody.

15.03.2005 Uyalar opens! The first free hosting made in Irad. Sign up, choose your address and build your nest. The first hundred nests will be in the catalogue on the main page.
