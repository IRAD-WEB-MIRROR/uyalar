salam, dreamer ☁

This is a dream dictionary. Not mine: my grandmother's. Saliyka apa is eighty and has read the dreams of everyone in our building since 1979, at the kitchen table, with tea. Since 2019 I write down what she says in a green notebook. The notebook is full, so here it is on the internet, sorted from A to Z in our alphabet, with Russian for babushka Valya upstairs and English for everyone else.

«Tüşüñ jakşılykka!»"may your dream be for good!", what apa says every morning before good morning

It is a reference site, not a diary. If you want to know what your dream means, look it up, or write in the guestbook and I'll ask her on Sunday.
