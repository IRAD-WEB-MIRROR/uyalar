Rules of the club «Sazan»

  1. A member respects the river. Take your rubbish home.
  2. No nets, no electricity, no explosives. Who does this is out of the club the same day.
  3. Small fish goes back into the water. A sazan under 1 kg is a child.
  4. No vodka on the boat. On the shore, after, as decided by the elders.
  5. The gun is carried in a case and unloaded in the car. Everyone has the papers.
  6. The duty of the month: petrol, the boat, the key of the garage.
